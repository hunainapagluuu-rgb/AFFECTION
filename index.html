Mere andar ek ajeeb si khwahish hai tumhe apne lafzon mein describe karne ki…

Itni khoobsurti se ki jo bhi in pages ko padhe, uske andar ek halki si curiosity reh jaaye tumhe dekhne ki.

Jab Aap Paas Nahi Thi, Aur Dil Khaali Sa Tha

Meri jaan, aapke birthday par mujhe pata chala ki aapki kami kaisi hoti hai.

Meri jaan, aaj aapka birthday tha, aur aaj aap apni duniya mein thi, apne logon ke beech, apni khushiyon mein. Mujhe is baat se kabhi shikaayat nahi ho sakti. Aapko hansta hua dekhna mujhe hamesha sukoon deta hai, chahe woh hansi mere saath ho ya mere bina. Lekin aaj ke din mujhe ek aisi baat pata chali jo shayad main pehle kabhi lafzon mein nahi keh paaya tha.

Aaj aapne mujhse zyada baat nahi ki. Shayad aap busy thi, shayad aapko waqt nahi mila, aur main yeh samajhta hoon. Aapka din aapke apne logon ka tha, aur unka haq bhi tha. Phir bhi, meri jaan, din ke har ghante mein mera dil bas ek hi cheez dhoondhta raha: aapka ek message, aapki ek chhoti si awaaz, aapka ek “main yahin hoon.” Maine kitni baar phone uthaya, kitni baar chat kholi, aur kitni baar bina kuch dekhe wapas rakh diya. Har baar dil ne yahi socha ki shayad ab aayega, shayad ab aap yaad karengi. Aur jab nahi aaya, toh ek chhoti si chubhan dil ke andar baith gayi, jo din ke saath badhti gayi.

Mujhe nahi pata tha ki ek aam sa din bhi itna lamba ho sakta hai. Mujhe nahi pata tha ki kisi ki khamoshi itni gehri ho sakti hai, jab woh khamoshi us insaan ki ho jiske lafz aapke poore din ko rang dete hon. Aapke bina har ghanta bhaari laga, har pal adhoora laga, aur main sabke beech hote hue bhi akela sa mehsoos karta raha.

Aaj mujhe aisa laga jaise mere andar se kuch kam ho gaya ho. Koi cheez jo roz chupchaap apni jagah par rehti thi, aur aaj achanak khaali thi. Main logon ke beech tha, baatein bhi kar raha tha, kabhi kabhi muskura bhi raha tha, lekin andar kahin ek udaasi baithi rahi jo kisi ko dikhaayi nahi di. Aisi udaasi jiski wajah main kisi ko samjha bhi nahi sakta tha, kyunki wajah sirf ek thi: aap.

Main bahut udaas tha, meri jaan. Aur main chahta hoon ki aap yeh samjhein ki yeh udaasi kisi shikaayat se nahi aayi thi. Aapne kuch galat nahi kiya. Yeh udaasi isliye thi ki aaj ke din main aapke sabse zyada paas hona chahta tha, aur aaj hi sabse zyada door mehsoos hua. Jis din main aapko sabse zyada khush dekhna chahta tha, usi din main aapki khushi ka hissa nahi ban paaya, aur shayad yahi baat mere dil ko sabse zyada chubhi.

Mujhe lagta hai kuch log hamari zindagi mein aise aa jaate hain ki unki jagah kabhi koi aur le hi nahi sakta. Jab woh paas hote hain toh hum yeh mehsoos hi nahi karte ki hum unpar kitna depend karne lage hain. Lekin jab ek din unki awaaz nahi aati, tab ehsaas hota hai ki woh humare din ki aadat nahi, humari dhadkan ka hissa ban chuke hain. Aaj mujhe wahi ehsaas hua.

Shayad mohabbat sirf saath rehne mein nahi hoti. Shayad woh un khaali lamhon mein bhi hoti hai jab hum kisi ko itna yaad karte hain ki dil bhaari ho jaaye aur aankhein bina wajah nam ho jaayein. Aaj mujhe samajh aaya ki aap mere liye sirf ek aadat nahi, ek zaroorat ban chuki hain. Aisi zaroorat jo bina maange dil mein jagah bana leti hai, aur jiske na hone par duniya thodi si adhoori lagti hai.

Main aapse yeh nahi keh raha ki aap mere liye apni khushiyan chhod dein. Main chahta hoon ki aap hamesha hanste rahein, apne logon ke saath, apni duniya mein, bina kisi guilt ke. Main bas itna chahta hoon ki aap jaanein ki jab aap hansti hain, toh meri duniya roshan hoti hai, aur jab aap door hoti hain, toh meri duniya thodi si khaamosh ho jaati hai.

Aaj ka din guzar gaya, aur main aapse naaraaz nahi hoon. Mere dil mein aapke liye sirf pyaar hai, aur bas itna kehna tha ki aapke bina yeh din kuch adhoora sa tha. Meri bas ek chhoti si dua hai: aane wale har birthday par, aap chahe kitni bhi busy ho ya kitni bhi door, aapko yeh yaad rahe ki koi hai jo aapko dil se yaad karta hai, aapke ek message ka pyaar se intezaar karta hai, aur aapke bina bhi aapko apni har dua mein saath rakhta hai.

Aapke Saath, Zindagi Ke Har Aam Din Mein

Meri jaan, aaj aapka din hai — aur meri dua aapke har aane wale kal ke liye hai.

Meri jaan, aaj aapka din hai. Aaj ki tareekh aapki zindagi ka ek aur khoobsurat safha ban kar aayi hai. Shayad aaj aapko bahut saari duayein milengi, bahut saare log aapko muskuraane ki wajah denge, aur bahut se log aapko bataayenge ki aap unke liye kitni khaas hain. Lekin aaj main aapse kuch alag kehna chahta hoon. Main aapko sirf yeh nahi batana chahta ki aap mere liye kitni khaas hain. Main aapko yeh batana chahta hoon ki aapke saath apni zindagi ke baare mein sochna mere dil ko kaisa sukoon deta hai.

Kyuki meri jaan, aap mere liye sirf ek khoobsurat ehsaas nahi hain. Aap woh shakhs hain jinke saath main zindagi ke un lamhon ko bhi jeena chahta hoon jinhein duniya aam keh kar guzar jaati hai.

Main nahi jaanta ki aane wala har din kaisa hoga, meri jaan. Lekin main jaanta hoon ki aapke liye mere dil mein jo dua hai, woh sirf aaj ke liye nahi hai.

Main aapki aane wali har khushi ke liye dua karna chahta hoon. Aapke har naye khwaab ke liye, aapki har nayi manzil ke liye, aapke har us din ke liye jab aapko khud par fakhr mehsoos ho. Jab aap zulfein sanwaarti hain, kya woh bhi apna noor kam hone se darta hai
Aapki aankhon mein aisi gehraai hai ke samandar bhi kinaare dhoondhne lage
Aap ek baar nazar utha kar dekh lein toh khamosh lafz bhi ghazlein kehne lage

Aapki palkon ke saaye mein jaane kitne khwaab panaah paate honge
Aap jab nazrein jhukaati hongi, sitaaron ke dil bhi dhadak jaate honge
Aapke chehre par jab muskurahat apna aashiyaana banaati hai
Toh lagta hai jaise kisi udaas dua ko uski manzil mil jaati hai

Aapke hothon ki hansi mein koi aisa jaadu zaroor chhupa hoga
Warna aapko soch kar bhi mere dil ka mausam kaise badla hoga
Aap chalti hain toh lagta hai hawa bhi aahista chalna seekh gayi
Aap hansti hain toh lagta hai bahaar har mausam mein rehna seekh gayi

Magar aapka husn sirf aapke chehre ki lakeeron mein kahaan qaid hai
Woh toh aapke lehje ki narmi mein, aapki rooh mein, aapke wajood mein shaamil hai
Main chaand likhun toh aapki aankhon ki chamak adhoori reh jaati hai
Main phool likhun toh aapki muskurahat ki khushboo lafzon se pare reh jaati hai

Main sitaaron se misaal loon toh aapki saadgi ka haq ada nahi hota
Main duniya ki saari khoobsurati likh doon, phir bhi aapka zikr poora nahi hota
Khuda ne husn likha hoga toh aapka khayaal bhi aaya hoga
Tabhi toh ek chehre ko dekh kar kisi ne poori zindagi likhne ka khwaab sajaaya hoga.

Aur aakhir mein, meri jaan…

Aaj aapse baat kam hui, toh socha apne dil ki baat in pannon par utaar doon. Shayad aapko kabhi andaaza bhi na ho ki aapke saath guzre chand lamhe mere liye kitni badi daulat hain. Main aapse har waqt baat karne ka haq nahi maangta, main bas chahta hoon ki jab kabhi aapko meri yaad aaye, toh aapke dil mein yeh khayaal zaroor aaye ki duniya ke kisi kone mein ek shakhs hai jo aapki ek chhoti si muskurahat ko bhi apne din ki sabse khoobsurat jeet samajhta hai. Jo aapko sirf aapke achhe dinon mein nahi, balki aapki khamoshiyon, aapki uljhanon aur aapke un dinon mein bhi chahta hai jab aapko khud se bhi mohabbat karna mushkil lagta ho.

Aap meri pasand ka koi hissa nahi hain, meri jaan. Aap woh poori kitaab hain jise padhne ke baad bhi mera dil kehta hai — abhi toh mohabbat ki shuruaat hui hai.
